## Summary

This plan outlines the detailed execution strategy for writing and organizing the "Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics" book. It defines a clear, step-by-step approach, breaking the book creation into distinct phases, milestones, and writing tasks, adhering to principles of book-first development, clear progression from basics to advanced topics, modular writing, and educational clarity, ensuring each step produces readable content.

**Performance Goals**: N/A (performance for content creation is not applicable; Docusaurus static site performance is handled by the framework).

**Scale/Scope**: A multi-part educational book covering Physical AI and Humanoid Robotics concepts.

## Writing Order Rules
- Follow phases in sequential order (Phase 1 then Phase 2, etc.)
- Finish all chapters within one part before moving to the next part
- Each chapter must be self-contained in its explanation
- Introduce concepts before providing detailed examples
- Avoid implementation details unless absolutely necessary for conceptual understanding

## Quality Rules
- **Clarity**: Use simple and clear English, avoiding jargon where possible or explaining it thoroughly.
- **Concept-Focused**: Prioritize the explanation of core concepts.
- **Beginner-Friendly**: Content should be accessible to learners with basic programming knowledge.
- **Conciseness**: Avoid unnecessary depth or overly complex explanations.
- **Consistency**: Maintain consistent terminology, formatting, and tone throughout the book.
- **Educational Tone**: Adopt an instructive and helpful tone.

## Validation Rules
- Every chapter and section MUST align with the project constitution and the feature specification.
- No new scope or topics outside the defined curriculum are allowed.
- Strictly no content related to RAG or chatbots (beyond their conceptual exclusion) will be included.
- The book content must be able to stand alone as a comprehensive educational resource.

## Authority
This plan governs the execution of the book writing process. All tasks related to content creation, organization, and structuring must strictly adhere to the guidelines and phases defined in this plan.
